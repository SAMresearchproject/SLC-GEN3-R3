# Public disclosure policy

## Included

- canonical mathematical exposition for four volumes and methodology;
- compact definitions, identities, result classifications, exact open
  boundaries, meaningful failures, corrections, and successor logic;
- public-safe conceptual interfaces for SLC, W8/X1/W9, W9P, Theta18, AFC,
  Exact Write, the RH adapter, and source transversality;
- hashes, repository-relative source locators, public commit pins, evidence
  records, and deterministic validation tools;
- the published source-transversality manuscript source and its publication
  custody record.

## Excluded

- private physical-SLC construction details, operational know-how, and
  unpublished implementation internals;
- patents, provisional filings, counsel work, privileged strategy, and
  stewardship-control legal documents;
- credentials, personal data, absolute machine paths, caches, virtual
  environments, IDE state, raw bulk ledgers, and private working notes;
- withdrawn RH bundle 0010 and prepared-but-unpublished bundle 0016;
- stale local-public-repository state as a substitute for the verified public
  commit;
- executable RH-Q2/SLC internals beyond the public mathematical interface.

## Rule

The atlas may say what an interface means, what evidence supports it, and
where a public source is pinned. It does not need to expose every implementation
detail that produced the source record. If a future addition changes this
boundary, it requires an explicit disclosure decision recorded in the
migration and source-custody registries.

## License boundary

The [SAMA license](LICENSE.md) applies only to material actually included and
owned or licensable here. It does not convert an excluded item into a public
disclosure, grant SAM/SLC invention or physical-implementation patent rights,
or relicense a source snapshot or third-party work. Any express AGPL patent
terms for covered software remain intact. Conversely, the protected-material
exclusions do not narrow the
`CC-BY-SA-4.0` permissions granted for original public atlas content or the
`AGPL-3.0-or-later` permissions granted for SAMA software.
