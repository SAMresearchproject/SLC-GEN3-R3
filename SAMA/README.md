# Substrate Accumulation Model

Supporting research and data for [SLC-GEN3-R3](../README.md).
See [current project status](../docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md) for the latest engine
and research information.

The Substrate Accumulation Model (SAM) begins with the idea that matter displaces
the substrate and that displacement accumulates. The research develops this
idea through the accumulation field, matter, and exact computation.

Read Volume I to begin with the model. Each volume contains branches with the
explanation, mathematical development and related tests together.

| Volume | What you will find |
|---|---|
| [Volume I — Substrate and accumulation](vol_i/README.md) | Foundations, gravity, clocks, light, black holes, cosmology and galaxies. |
| [Volume II — Matter](vol_ii/README.md) | Matter foundations, particles, nuclei, elements, binding and carriers. |
| [Volume III — Computation](vol_iii/README.md) | SAM language, the Substrate Ledger Computer, Writes, history and applications. |
| [Volume IV — Riemann Hypothesis](vol_iv/README.md) | The RH question, source and sign arguments, carriers, results and corrections. |

Within a branch, start with its README and follow the chapters in order. Open
its `tests/` folder to see the associated questions, calculations, recorded
outcomes and original sources.
