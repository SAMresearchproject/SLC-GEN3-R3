## Formula surface

```text
R = 12
D = 3
alpha_H = 2
A_side = 1/24
A_share = 1/12
c0_SAM = -0.5 * ln(23/24)
t_fire = coherence_time * c0_SAM
textbook proxy = coherence_time * 0.5
uniform proxy residual = abs(c0_SAM - 0.5) / 0.5
```

CR073a reports c0_SAM = 0.021279807209397945 and c0_textbook = 0.5. The residual is uniform because both predictors are linear in coherence time; the difference is in the constants, not in row-specific fitting.
