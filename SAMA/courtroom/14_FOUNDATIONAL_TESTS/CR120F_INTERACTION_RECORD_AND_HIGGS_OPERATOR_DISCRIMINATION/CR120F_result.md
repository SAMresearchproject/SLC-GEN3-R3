## Operator discrimination

normalization check:

```text

```

it changes total trace and is not trace-preserving. At `w = 1` it is only the
identity channel, with no conditional outcomes and no record creation.

Therefore a scalar Higgs weight cannot, by itself, be the complete
information-writing operator. A scalar could still parameterize a separately
defined Hamiltonian or channel; that possibility remains open.
