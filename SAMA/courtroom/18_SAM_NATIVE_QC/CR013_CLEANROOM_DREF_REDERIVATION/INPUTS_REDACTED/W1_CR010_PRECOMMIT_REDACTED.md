## §1 The visualization, stated formally

**§1.1 The substrate is a 2D closed-loop lattice.** The substrate is a two-dimensional manifold populated by closed loops. Each closed loop is a substrate primitive — a bounded, non-self-intersecting circuit through the 2D substrate medium. The lattice of loops fills the substrate plane. In Sean Brady's working shorthand, each loop is called a *spaghettio*, and the substrate as a whole is the *spaghettio lattice* or *ice-sheet*.

**§1.2 Loop scale identification with the heaviest neutrino Compton wavelength.** The characteristic size of a substrate closed loop is identified with the Compton wavelength of the heaviest neutrino mass eigenstate `m_3`, per CR268 (tensor 6 = ĥ·d̂ heaviest-neutrino identification):

```text
λ_spaghettio  ≡  λ_C(m_3)  =  ħc / (m_3·c²)
              =  197.327 MeV·fm / 50.9 meV
              =  3.877 μm     (SI, per SAM_ON_EARTH_v1.md)
```

The identification is not a fit. It is the composition of CR268's sealed identification `m_3 substrate value = ĥ·d̂ = 6` with the Compton composition of the corresponding physical mass eigenstate (`m_3 = 50.9 meV/c²` per CR001@20 spectrum anchored to Δm²_31 measurement) via fundamental constants read in SI per the frame anchor. The loop size is not chosen; it is what the sealed substrate says it is.

**§1.3 Matter is a rigid geometric shape (or stack thereof) propagating against the substrate.** Matter, in the ontology, is not a point particle and not an open string alone; it is a rigid geometric shape or stack of shapes, propagating through the substrate lattice. Sean Brady's working shorthand names the unit *MeV toothpick*; a particle is a stack of toothpicks composing a stable shape at certain quantities. Only some stable shapes structurally link with substrate carriers (CR262 carrier set {6, 9, 18, 54}) and thereby couple to the substrate at all.

Note: the MeV toothpick unit is Sean-declared as conceptual; the specific mass value per toothpick and the stack-arithmetic mapping to physical particles are not sealed here. What is sealed is the *ontological character*: matter is rigid, extended geometrically, and propagates against a spaghettio-lattice substrate. The MeV-scale identification is a candidate structural claim carried forward for its own future CR.

**§1.4 Matter-substrate contact pops closed loops open.** When a rigid matter shape contacts a substrate spaghettio, the loop is broken open at the contact point. The spaghettio ceases to be a closed loop; both endpoints of the broken strand become mobile. In Sean Brady's shorthand: it *pops*.

**§1.5 Popped strands bounce and intersect.** The broken-strand endpoints propagate through the surrounding spaghettio lattice ("bounce") until they intersect endpoints of other popped strands elsewhere on the substrate. The intersection pattern of these bounced endpoints encodes structural information into the substrate.

**§1.6 3D reality is holographic from the intersection pattern.** The 3D reality that instruments measure — physical distances, spatial extents, material configurations — is the holographic projection of the 2D intersection pattern of popped-and-bounced spaghettio strands. The holography is *not* optical interference (Sean Brady's explicit qualifier: "not like we think with lasers, that's the best analogy I have"); it is *intersection-density holography* — 3D reality is a readout of intersection density on the 2D substrate manifold.

**§1.7 Physical distance is information-only.** Under this ontology, physical distance is not a fundamental quantity but an emergent readout of intersection density. Zero physical distance between two objects does not mean zero substrate structure between them; it means the intersection density along the connecting path is such that the 3D holographic readout returns "zero separation." Conversely, billions of light-years of physical distance can correspond to any spaghettio-lattice configuration whose intersection density holographically encodes that separation.

**§1.8 Nested Homes take zero physical space.** A Home (A=1 completed surface, per Vol I §7 and CR003@19) is a substrate structure whose physical space is entirely information. Trillions of Homes nested within a parent Home, and that Home within its parent, and so on outward — all occupy zero physical space in the strict measurement sense, because physical space itself is emergent from the substrate. The nested-Home ontology is consistent with quantum spaghettification (§2 below) at the horizon boundary and with the CR003@19 A=1=A₀ identity that seals the Home-to-fresh-A₀ typed reset.

## §2 Quantum spaghettification as internal consistency witness

Sean Brady coined the term *quantum spaghettification* prior to CR003@19's sealing of the A=1=A₀ Home typed-reset. The mechanism, in the spaghettio-lattice ontology:

- An object approaches a Home (extreme accumulation gradient).
- Front-to-back the object's substrate spaghettios unsync — the leading edge samples more accumulation-inflated substrate than the trailing edge.
- The object is pulled apart pixel-by-pixel (spaghettio-by-spaghettio) as the front and back cease to occupy consistent substrate structure.
- The disassembled object arrives at the horizon boundary as plasma.
- At horizon, the only stable matter configuration is hydrogen (simplest stable rigid-shape stack per §1.3).
- The horizon receives hydrogen.

**Internal consistency claim (not gated as a numerical test here, cited as evidence for §1's coherence):** the same pop-bounce-intersect mechanism (§1.4-1.6) applies across intensity regimes. One mechanism, multiple intensity regimes, all internally consistent with sealed observables.

This dual-scale coherence of a single mechanism is a *witness* to the ontology's structural integrity. Ontologies that reach across scales without adjustment are the shape of load-bearing physical principles; ontologies that require different mechanisms at different scales are the shape of decorative metaphor.

## §3 Structural claims sealed by this CR

Six load-bearing claims:

**S1.** The substrate is a 2D closed-loop lattice (§1.1). Downstream artifacts assume 2D substrate topology and closed-loop structure.

**S2.** Substrate closed-loop scale is identified with the Compton wavelength of the heaviest neutrino mass eigenstate `m_3 = 50.9 meV/c²`, giving `λ_spaghettio = 3.877 μm` in SI (§1.2). Downstream artifacts use this scale.

**S3.** Matter is rigid-geometric (or stacked rigid-geometric) propagating against the substrate (§1.3). Matter-substrate coupling is contact-based, not field-permeating.

**S4.** Matter-substrate contact pops a closed loop open at the contact point (§1.4). Substrate write events are loop-breaking events, not amplitude-modulation events.

**S5.** Popped-strand endpoints bounce through the lattice and intersect other popped-strand endpoints; the intersection pattern encodes information (§1.5-1.6). Coupling between two matter locations is via intersection density between their respective popped-strand ensembles.

**S6.** Physical distance is emergent from intersection density; nested Homes taking zero physical space are consistent (§1.7-1.8). R-power dressings in downstream coupling laws are structural consequences of Home-nesting projection levels, not device parameters.
