# CR284 Water-Ion Shape Selectivity Campaign Plan

## Program Objective

Determine whether the SAM/QP construction grammar adds predictive information
about water-ion structure and nanoscale-pore selectivity beyond ordinary
descriptors, then separately determine whether structured vibration improves a
real desalination gate per unit total energy.

The program seeks a practical water benefit. It does not seek unlimited energy,
sound-only desalination, or a post-hoc interpretation of known membrane data.

## Typed Bridge

```text
QP093A typed motif candidate
-> species construction candidate
-> hydrated-complex construction candidate
-> pore-shape compatibility score
-> deformation / de-coordination ordering
-> blind ion and pore ranking
-> external molecular reveal
-> physical coupon only if predictive gates pass
```

This bridge is a candidate construction. QP093A geometry remains physically
open. SAM carrier/container roles are not dissolved ions or semiconductor
carriers. Starbreaker is process-context only: dimensionless `A` is not
pressure, energy density, flow, chemical potential, or specific energy without
a separately sourced typed operator.

## Lane A - Shape Selectivity

Lane A freezes a construction before reference values are opened.

Required prediction fields:

- preferred construction family;
- candidate coordination count or count range;
- maximum cross-section ordering;
- support or closure requirement;
- pore compatibility ordering;
- number of coordination contacts displaced at a constriction;
- deformation/de-coordination sequence;
- relative barrier class, never an invented SI energy.

Lane A must beat, on held-out species and pores:

- bare-ion radius;
- hydrated radius;
- formal charge;
- charge-density proxy;
- conventional coordination number;
- pore diameter alone;
- randomized shape assignment.

## Lane B - Assisted Gate

Lane B inherits SCD-01 through SCD-04:

```text
structured vibration = opportunity / boundary-layer organization
real gate            = membrane + electric field, seed, or ice front
committed separation = measured product and concentrate streams
primary metric       = grams NaCl removed per total watt-hour
```

The first physical direction remains low-voltage electrodialysis with matched
gate-only, random-helper, ordinary-spacer, selected-spacer, helper-only,
heat-only, and mass-balance controls. Lane B may proceed independently if Lane
A fails, but it cannot be described as validation of Lane A.

## Process and Cost Boundary

A useful desalination result must eventually report ordinary units:

- product-water salinity and recovery;
- water and salt mass balance;
- voltage, current, acoustic input, pumping, heating/cooling, and total energy;
- grams salt removed per Wh;
- kWh per cubic metre at a stated product-water target;
- membrane area, flow, pressure, temperature, pH, and run duration;
- fouling, scaling, cleaning recovery, and material lifetime.

Starbreaker may later motivate a loading hypothesis, but it supplies none of
these physical quantities in CR284.

## Reveal Discipline

1. Freeze the species roster and allowed chemical inputs.
2. Freeze the construction algorithm and all tie-break rules.
3. Emit signed/hashed predictions for the complete roster.
4. Freeze a pore panel without observed rejection labels.
5. Score held-out rankings against simple baselines.
6. Only then open the external molecular and experimental reference values.
7. Preserve failures, boundary rows, and post-reveal correction proposals as
   new records; never overwrite the original predictions.

## First Falsifier

The shape-selectivity claim fails as an advantage if it cannot outperform the
simple baselines on held-out species and pores, or if it needs a new support
rule, coefficient, or geometry after each reveal.

The assisted-gate claim fails if structured vibration does not improve salt
removed per total Wh relative to both gate-only and power-matched random-helper
controls after heat, evaporation, misting, contamination, and mass balance are
closed.

## Campaign Stop Rules

- Stop Lane A at `BOUNDARY_NO_PREDICTIVE_EDGE` if the blind score does not beat
  the declared baselines.
- Stop Lane B at `BOUNDARY_NO_ENERGY_NORMALIZED_GAIN` if the physical helper
  does not beat active controls.
- Use `FAIL_MEASUREMENT_UNCLOSED` whenever salt, water, or energy balance is
  incomplete.
- Use `DISCOVERY_QUEUE` only for a repeatable unexplained effect; it is not a
  PASS verdict.
- No physical or humanitarian deployment claim is permitted before independent
  replication and water-quality/safety review.
