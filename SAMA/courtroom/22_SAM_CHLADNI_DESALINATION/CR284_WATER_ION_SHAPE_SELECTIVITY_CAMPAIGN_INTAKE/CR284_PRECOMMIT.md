## Observation-Blind Rule

Before CR286 predictions are sealed, the construction lane may use only:

- chemical identity and formal charge;
- the frozen QP093A/SAM construction grammar listed in the input manifest;
- the precommitted pore-schema fields;
- algebraic and geometric consistency checks.

It may not use measured or simulated hydrated radii, dehydration free energies,
transport barriers, permeability, rejection, flux, or observed selectivity to
choose a shape, support rule, coefficient, or pore ranking.

The external papers listed in `CR284_external_sources.csv` are metadata and
benchmark-design context at intake. Their numerical tables and species-level
results belong to the later reveal gate.

boric acid and borate because the chemical species is pH-dependent; the
campaign may not hide that dependence inside a fitted geometry rule.
