# CR284 Water Stewardship Intent

## Recorded Intent

Sean Brady has directed that if this program produces a reproducible
improvement in water desalination, the improvement is to be given away for
humanitarian use rather than held as an exclusive private advantage.

For this campaign, that means:

- result selection is not conditioned on commercial exclusivity;
- protocols, negative results, wrong controls, and sufficient reproduction
  detail are intended for public release;
- a validated useful design is intended to receive a royalty-free,
  non-exclusive humanitarian release mechanism;
- access for water-stressed and low-resource communities is a primary design
  criterion, not an afterthought;
- safety, water quality, environmental effects, and independent replication
  remain mandatory even when the design is freely shared.

## Legal Boundary

This record preserves technical and stewardship intent. It does not itself
select or create an operative copyright license, patent pledge, hardware
license, standards commitment, warranty, regulatory approval, or legal entity
obligation. Those instruments must be prepared explicitly before public
release and must preserve the recorded non-exclusive humanitarian intent.

## Scientific Boundary

The humanitarian purpose does not lower the evidence threshold. A model PASS
is not clean water, and an inexpensive prototype is not safe drinking water.
Failed and boundary results remain part of the public record so others do not
repeat avoidable errors.
