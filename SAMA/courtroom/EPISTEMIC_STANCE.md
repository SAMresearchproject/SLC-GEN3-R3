# Epistemic Stance

SAM is built on bold and revolutionary ideas. The author's working principle
is that this is what makes it correct.

Most physics frameworks try to be safe. They avoid claims that could be
falsified by next year's measurement and fit existing data with adjustable
parameters. SAM does the opposite. It commits to structural claims that a
safer framework would never make:

```text
no interior to a black hole; matter trapped on the inner face of a closed sphere
the Higgs split gives gravity, not mass; mass was always in the closed loop
the cosmological energy budget emits from pi, 2, and 3 with zero fitted parameters
Hawking radiation as pair-write Born rule biased by a forbidden inward option
the Big Bang as a previous universe's horizon inverting
a measurable gravitational decoherence floor for every quantum system
particles are routes that closed; quantum information is the same grammar held open
```

Then it tests those claims under strict zero-free-parameter discipline:
sealed predictions, hash-locked priority, wrong controls, explicit
falsification statements per CR, BOUNDARY verdicts where evidence is
insufficient, deferred-support appeal where downstream evidence earns the
upgrade without rewriting history.

The boldness and the discipline are not in tension. They reinforce each
other:

```text
A timid framework with adjustable parameters survives any data, says little.
A bold framework with no free parameters lives or dies by whether its
structural commitments turn out to be true.

The discipline is only meaningful if the claims are bold enough to be falsified.
The boldness is only credible if the discipline is strict enough to prevent retrofitting.
```

The framework's lineage is Tesla, not Edison. The Tesla path costs more in
the short run and means more in the long view. SAM chooses it knowing the
cost.

This is not a license to overclaim. Strict scope boundaries, BOUNDARY
verdicts, and "what this does not show" sections in every branch are all in
service of staying honest about how bold any given claim is. Bold structural
claims are made under the same discipline that conservative empirical claims
are made under; the difference is in the shape of the claim, not in the
standard it is held to.

If the framework is wrong, it will be wrong in ways that are decisive and
visible -- not in ways that get rescued by adjustable parameters. That is
the price of boldness, and the framework pays it willingly.
