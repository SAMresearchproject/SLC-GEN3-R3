# QP particle grammar semantic boundaries

The theorem is complete only for the finite structural grammar under the frozen
QP source axioms.

## Backed scoped language authority

- the eight-label alphabet;
- the seven constructor classes and source-defined terminals;
- exact constructor counts;
- exact native formulas;
- canonical ordered-pair and unordered-triad semantics;
- the 107/13 finite triad extension given typed observed-surface and tensor-
  support inputs;
- the 285/14/1/21 role partition;
- strict non-coercion among payload, infrastructure, global, and rejected roles.

## Conditional annotation

CB2250 may annotate content vertices and relation classes. It is not all-12
unique, does not decide legality, and does not establish physical geometry.

## Inherited SAM semantics with executable registration open

- completed resolved interaction as a ledger write;
- substrate as ledger medium;
- reciprocal contact with oriented views;
- stable/persistent identity rather than raw transcript identity;
- Starbreaker as remnant-producing formation context;
- isotope inventory, surface debit, lift, support, closure, and binding readout
  architecture.

These are existing repo concepts with source-specific grades. The missing
language operations are the lineage, physical write/site, Starbreaker
transition, terminal-remnant, and remnant-decoder welds.

## Physical assignment open

- unique physical entity identity;
- Starbreaker-generated dynamical ledger incidence;
- physical edge or face realization;
- physical mass;
- binding validation.

Incidence may not be produced by static template-to-slot placement or by an
isotope-label-driven minimum-cost geometry selector. It must be emitted by a
causal Starbreaker trajectory. Formation release and state binding remain
distinct unless separately frozen reference conditions equate them.

The current triad predicates are an extensional equivalence class on the frozen
120-candidate universe. The canonical two-feature guard is retained because it
preserves the source typing, not because the data uniquely identify it as a
mechanism.
