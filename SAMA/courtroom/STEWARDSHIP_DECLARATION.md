# SAM Research Project LC — Stewardship Declaration

**Document version:** v1.0

**Entity:** SAM Research Project LC
**Supersedes status:** This declaration sharpens and operationalizes the intent recorded in [STEWARDSHIP.md](STEWARDSHIP.md) (v0, 2026-06-08). The v0 statement remains in the repository as historical record; this v1 declaration is the binding instrument going forward.

## §1 — Purpose

This declaration binds the disposition of any commercial value that arises from the Substrate Accumulation Model (SAM), the Substrate Ledger Computer (SLC), or any work, apparatus, method, invention, or derivative enabled by them.

The intent is unambiguous and is not optional: if SAM-derived work generates commercial revenue or commercial equity, a material portion of that value carries public responsibility with it. The decision to direct that portion to public benefit is not contingent on future negotiation. It is a condition built into the work at origin and carried by every commercial party that subsequently holds, licenses, or commercializes the work.

## §2 — Binding parties

- **Originator and primary inventor:** Sean Brady
- **Holding entity:** SAM Research Project LC
- **Successors and assignees:** Any party that comes to hold, control, or commercialize SAM-derived work — by license, sale, merger, acquisition, equity placement, IPO, asset purchase, patent transfer, government action, or any other instrument — accepts this declaration as a condition of holding those rights. A party that does not accept this declaration is not eligible to hold or commercialize the work.

## §3 — Stewardship principle

This declaration governs the disposition of **commercial value received by SAM Research Project LC and its successor entities** as consideration for use, license, sale, or commercialization of SAM-derived work. It does not purport to govern the gross revenue, operating accounts, or tax treatment of any licensee, customer, or downstream commercial party. The licensee's payment terms are negotiated in the commercial instrument; this declaration governs what happens to those payments once SAM Research Project LC receives them.

For any commercial value received — including royalties, license fees, patent assignment proceeds, sale or acquisition proceeds, equity sale proceeds, dividends from controlled subsidiaries, settlement awards, or comparable inbound value — **no less than ninety percent (90%) of net proceeds** flows to public-benefit purposes named in §4.

"Net proceeds" means commercial value received by SAM Research Project LC (or successor entity holding the SAM IP) after reasonable and documented operating costs of the holding entity, applicable taxes on those receipts, and good-faith reinvestment in continued R&D have been deducted. "Net proceeds" does not permit deductions designed to dilute the stewardship flow — including inflated executive compensation, related-party fees, intra-group transfer pricing, or accounting recharacterizations moving ordinary inflows out of the stewardship base. Audit rights on the holding entity's books vest in the entity controlling the stewardship.

The Foundation funds its own operations from the stewardship flow it receives. A separate carve-out for Foundation overhead is not needed and not granted.

A commercial party that pays SAM Research Project LC for use of SAM-derived work knows in advance that no less than ninety percent of what they pay (net of the holding entity's reasonable costs) flows to the people the stewardship is intended to serve. That distribution is the price of using SAM. It is not waivable by side agreement and is not subject to renegotiation downward.

## §4 — The SAM Foundation

The intended vehicle for the stewardship flow is **the SAM Foundation**, to be established when commercial activity begins.

The Foundation's mission is to direct benefit to people who need help most, including communities outside the country of incorporation. Priority causes:

- **Housing:** homeless shelters, transitional housing, housing support services
- **Recovery:** addiction recovery and rehabilitation programs, peer support services
- **Food security:** food banks, food access infrastructure
- **Clean water:** well construction and water-access infrastructure, prioritizing communities without reliable potable water
- **Medical access:** charitable medical support for people who cannot afford care
- **Education and opportunity:** access programs for people whose path is otherwise blocked
- **Community charity and public good:** additional causes selected by the Foundation consistent with the founder's intent

SAM-derived value flows outward, not only upward.

If the SAM Foundation cannot be established for legal or operational reasons, the stewardship flow is directed through an equivalent public-benefit structure (donor-advised fund, charitable trust, named cause within an existing public-benefit organization, or international equivalent) selected by Sean Brady or his designated successor.

## §5 — Stewardship survival

This declaration survives, and is binding through:

- Sale or dissolution of SAM Research Project LC or any successor entity
- License grants of any scope (exclusive, non-exclusive, field-of-use, perpetual, term-limited)
- Acquisition or merger
- Equity placement, IPO, secondary sale, or other capital event
- Asset purchase
- Patent assignment, sale, or transfer
- Bankruptcy of any party in the chain of rights
- Change of control of any holding party
- Death, incapacity, or unavailability of Sean Brady or any named successor

Acquisition or merger proceeds received by Sean Brady, SAM Research Project LC's equity holders, or any successor entity's equity holders as consideration for transferring SAM-derived rights are commercial value within the meaning of §3 and are subject to the 90% net proceeds rule. The acquisition instrument must structure the transfer such that 90% of net acquisition proceeds flow to the Foundation; an acquisition that does not honor this requirement is not eligible to close.

A commercial party that violates the stewardship clause forfeits its license. Rights revert to the entity controlling the stewardship — Sean Brady, his designated successor, or the SAM Foundation once established. The reversion clause is enforceable independently of any other remedy.
