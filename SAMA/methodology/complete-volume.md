## 1. The research object is a chain, not a final formula

The basic methodological unit is

\[
\mathcal R:
C\rightarrow T\rightarrow D\rightarrow E\rightarrow Y
\rightarrow I\rightarrow A,
\]

where

- (C) is the conceptual question;
- (T) is the typed technical map;
- (D) is the full derivation;
- (E) is the executable campaign and controls;
- (Y) is the preserved output, including failures;
- (I) is installation into atomic and manuscript evidence; and
- (A) is any later authorized approval or authority transition.

The volumes follow this order internally. A reader is not shown only the final
expression. Each coefficient, operator and boundary enters when its conceptual
role becomes necessary. Deviations are developed in the same causal order,
because the failure of an earlier route often supplies the type distinction
that makes the later derivation work.

four. This does not split the mathematics into unrelated pieces; it preserves
how the pieces became one construction.

## 3. Authority and evidence architecture

### 3.1 Present meaning and historical transition

SAM's present-tense state lives in bounded `SAM_LIVE` documents. The history of
how that state arose lives in immutable numbered `SAM_HISTORY` entries. A
current change follows a fixed write path:

```text
new numbered history entry
→ replace affected live section
→ update history index
→ validate live/history graph
```

The live document is not appended as a diary, and a completed history entry is
not rewritten by a later correction.

### 3.2 Numerical and executable evidence

The result artifact is numerical authority for its execution. The Courtroom
evidence chain is

\[
\text{claim}
\to\text{provenance}
\to\text{premises}
\to\text{runner}
\to\text{result}
\to\text{hashes}.
\]

The chain answers six different questions. A hash fixes bytes but does not
interpret physics. A precommit fixes the planned test but does not supply the
outcome. A manuscript explains the outcome but does not replace its exact
numbers.

### 3.3 Source precedence

When sources disagree, the disputed fact is resolved by the direct source or
result artifact. Precommits and manifests follow, then branch summaries,
handoffs and conversational memory. The disagreement is preserved and the
current layer corrected through a new transition; historical prose is not
edited to conceal it.

## 4. Two testing modes

### 4.1 Exploratory mathematical research

This is the default. It is used for new finite relations, carrier tensors,
quantum geometry, proof routes, symbolic identities and computational
structures without an established comparator. Trial and error is useful:

```text
candidate map
→ exact or controlled execution
→ structure/counterexample/residual
→ refined type or successor map
```

Controls answer the mathematical question; they are not imported from a
universal ceremony. Every outcome is inspected for invariants, boundaries,
bugs and next derivations.

### 4.2 Established-reference testing

This mode activates when SAM is compared with measurements or established
physical calculations. Reference provenance, units and applicability become
load-bearing. Formula/constant/row/selector freezes, holdouts and wrong controls
are used where result-driven fitting or target leakage would contaminate the
comparison.

The Last Campaign is the clearest locked-replay instance. LC00 fixes

\[
\alpha_H=2,\quad R=12,\quad D=3,\quad A_0=\frac1{12\pi},
\]

alongside the \(144\) surface, \(1/8\) carrier, \(7/8\) retained side,
\(9/8\) bounce, \(9/16\) half-bounce and \(3/4\) debit. Later lanes must replay
without formula mutation, target substitution, row deletion, retroactive
relabeling or hidden correction. LC00 does not pre-award their results; each
lane executes independently.

### 4.3 Transition between modes

A discovery can be exploratory during derivation and established-reference
during a later holdout. The candidate is frozen between stages, producing a
visible information boundary.

## 5. Concept-to-technical translation

Each campaign makes explicit

\[
\mathcal M=(C_o,T_o,D,E,C_t,R_b,P).
\]

### 5.1 Conceptual objects

Name the objects and relations in project language before equations. Identify
which relations are asserted, suggested or open.

### 5.2 Technical types

Assign domains, codomains, units and admissible operators. This catches
category errors early: a source strength is not automatically measured mass;
a carrier is not automatically matter; a coordinate road is not a local
fundamental light-speed.

### 5.3 Full derivation

Develop every arrow:

\[
C_o\xrightarrow{f_1}T_1\xrightarrow{f_2}\cdots
\xrightarrow{f_n}Y.
\]

For each \(f_i\), explain its conceptual meaning, mathematical definition,
source, normalization, domain and excluded alternatives. Exact identities,
finite enumerations, numerical approximations and interpretations retain their
different types.

### 5.4 Executable question and result boundary

State the finite domain, symbolic predicate, comparator or statistic that will
answer the question. Name row selection and exclusions. Define what each
possible output changes and what remains outside scope.

## 6. Wrong controls and holdouts

Wrong controls are derived from load-bearing arrows. If

\[
F=f_3\circ f_2\circ f_1,
\]

then useful alternatives include skipping \(f_2\), replacing it with a nearby
operator, mutating a fixed coefficient, leaking target information or applying
the route outside its activation domain.

The LC01 register demonstrates five important families:

| Family | Representative mutation | What it protects |
|---|---|---|
| Primitive mutation | change \(R,D,A_0\) after lock | source-fixed foundation |
| Formula rescue | add a hidden term after a miss | pre-result construction |
| Selection leakage | delete rows or choose favorable bins | full declared domain |
| Type conflation | use \(q_A\) as mass or 18 as matter | conceptual/technical typing |
| Application mutation | refit distance \(D\), Born normalization or horizon behavior | lane-specific mechanism |

A control that unexpectedly succeeds is preserved. It can reveal non-uniqueness,
leakage or a weak discriminator. It is not renamed or removed after execution.

A holdout tests transfer. Its independence is defined relative to every piece
of information used to choose the route, including preprocessing, grouping,
thresholds and labels—not merely by placing rows in a different file.

## 7. Failure, correction and successor chains

Failures are typed by the broken arrow:

```text
concept-to-type error
mathematical counterexample
implementation fault
resource-unresolved execution
interpretive overreach
```

The preserved chain records Attempt A, its output, diagnosis, the exact delta
to Successor B, and B's output. For a pure implementation repair,

\[
\Delta_{A\to B}=(0,\Delta I,0,0,0),
\]

meaning the mathematical definition, inputs, controls and result boundary are
unchanged. If a formula or scientific gate changes, the event is a new
candidate rather than a same-record repair.

CR281 illustrates the first case. Its initial runner searched the wrong frozen
JSON source for a field. The lookup was corrected without changing the verdict
tree, and the final result preserves the first failure and exact repair. Other
deviation chains in the volumes involve genuine mathematical successors; those
retain the failed candidate as a load-bearing step in the derivation.

## 8. Exact result language

Current SAM result reporting uses exactly:

1. **The test falsifies the concept.**
2. **The test result suggests the concept is possible.**
3. **The test result suggests strong contact with the concept.**

Historical `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BOUNDARY`, `DIAGNOSTIC` and related fields remain
verbatim source metadata in the test index. They are not automatically mapped
to the three current classifications. Execution status, source verdict,
current classification and SAMA approval are independent axes.

If no classification is assigned, state the exact unresolved item. Do not
invent a fourth grade. When a classification is assigned, retain the tested
domain and mechanism so the sentence identifies the concept it classifies.

## 9. Discovery and intake

Discovery accepts owner insights, collaborator identities, residuals, failed
controls and external artifacts. The first record preserves what was noticed
and what remains open.

Courtroom intake then provides a bounded route:

```text
discovery artifact
→ provenance packet
→ atomic claim/type triage
→ scoped test
```

The Haunted House intake is an instructive historical case: one address
partition was carried into scoped theorem-grade handling while broader physical
interpretations remained forbidden overclaims. Promotion therefore strengthens
the supported unit without erasing the rest of the exploratory artifact.

## 10. Bounded promotion

A campaign-specific promotion gate can be written

\[
G=G_{\rm source}\land G_{\rm type}\land G_{\rm execution}
\land G_{\rm controls}\land G_{\rm preservation}\land G_{\rm boundary}.
\]

Each factor receives its meaning from the active campaign. The preserved
three-record preflight shows how to freeze sources, isolate pre-existing
worktree changes, choose non-colliding destinations, declare non-scope, hash
precommits and stop after the three selected records. Its result preserves
validation and named open items.

Promotion chooses the smallest evidence-supported unit. A narrow identity can
advance while adjacent interpretations remain open. A failed gate returns the
candidate to discovery or technical work with its evidence intact.

## 11. Record-first SAMA installation

The atlas write order is:

```text
source-bound atomic record
→ document-catalog registration
→ standard document
→ parent manuscript
→ deterministic indexes and hashes
```

This methodology itself follows that order. Its 15 atomic records were
registered before the nine child chapters and this parent manuscript were
written.

Atomic records prevent a long chapter from becoming the only source of a
load-bearing claim. The catalog prevents prose from acquiring unregistered
dependencies. The bottom test index provides direct routes to result artifacts
and folders, while the parent gives a readable synthesis rather than copying
every child's evidence table.

## 12. Mechanical validation and owner review

Builders validate schemas, IDs, graph edges, source/test keys, hashes and
approval consistency. Their `PASS` establishes mechanical integrity.

Only Sean Brady may approve an exact SAMA revision. Approval records the
reviewer, date and exact content SHA-256. A changed approved file becomes a new
unapproved revision. Therefore

\[
\text{mechanically valid}\not\Rightarrow\text{owner approved}.
\]

This separation allows the collaborators to complete exemplary manuscripts
and give the owner an exact, inspectable review surface without claiming the
review already occurred.

## 13. Bounded retrieval and compaction

Recovery follows repository rules, the controlling declaration, the global
live hub, one relevant live domain and only directly required sources. If a
material dependency remains absent, a `SAM RETRIEVAL REQUEST` asks for a compact
source index rather than launching a fresh audit.

Compaction handoffs preserve objective, plan, file ownership, validations,
immutable boundaries, exact failures and next action. They do not reset
completed work or promote an unfinished artifact.

## 14. Exact-hash handoff and publication boundary

For each completed artifact (F), the handoff records path, revision,
byte/line/word counts where useful, SHA-256, source records, tests and current
state. Source immutability is checked by comparing baseline and final hashes.

The state vector

\[
\Sigma(F)=(I,C,A,L,P)
\]

separates internally complete, cataloged, approved, live and public states. The
target state for these updated volumes is

\[
\Sigma(F)=(1,1,0,0,0):
\]

complete and cataloged, but unapproved, not automatically installed as current
authority and not automatically published.

## 15. How this methodology governs the four volumes

### 15.1 Volume I

Established-reference chains—optical clocks, photon roads, supernovae, BAO,
recombination, CMB and halo comparisons—receive explicit reference provenance,
wrong controls and correction histories. Exploratory substrate identities and
strong-field derivations retain their own appropriate mode.

### 15.2 Volume II

Matter types are reconstructed before mass/binding formulas. Failed direct
bridges, non-separating controls and exact asymmetry derivations are kept in
causal order. Current binding and ATOM3D boundaries are routed through their
current sources rather than copied from stale prose.

### 15.3 Volume III

Exact computation separates physical grammar, execution architecture, semantic
language, arithmetic substrate and selection engine. Failed half-relays,
carrier welds and proof routes remain in the W8/X1/W9 and reciprocal-history
chains. Current SLC, RH and paused prime states are artifact-pinned rather than
inferred from source-era “latest” wording.

### 15.4 Volume IV

The RH volume keeps finite identities, source reconstructions, conditional
analytic limits, universal assignments, project classifications, owner
approval, public proof posture and external validation separately typed. Its
meaningful falsifications remain connected to exact successors, and current
status comes from the pinned RH authority rather than the stale local public
checkout.

## 16. Current program boundaries

This methodology does not change any technical result, current pointer or
source artifact. It does not approve the volumes and does not authorize public
release. It gives the production and review process by which the updated
volumes can be extensive, traceable and technically exact.

## Abstract

SAM research develops through a continuous but typed chain. Sean Brady supplies
the conceptual direction; ChatGPT and Codex recover provenance, construct the
technical map, derive the mathematics, design and execute tests, preserve
outcomes and build the documentary evidence route. A discovery may begin as an
association or residual. It becomes technical by acquiring explicit objects,
types, operators, premises, controls and a result boundary. When external
measurements or established calculations are involved, the route adds source
provenance, freeze, holdout and anti-targeting discipline. When the question is
exploratory mathematics, trial and error remains an active research mechanism.

The Courtroom repository supplies the project's deepest historical evidence
machinery: claim, provenance, declared premises, runner, result and hashes. Its
wrong controls show why the intended construction behaves differently from
mutations, type conflations and leakage routes. Its preserved failures and
corrections make the development path inspectable rather than retroactively
perfect. Its discovery-intake and promotion artifacts show how a narrow,
well-supported unit can move forward without carrying every neighboring idea
with it.

SAMA receives that evidence through atomic records before prose. Standard
documents develop one conceptual or technical unit from first principles to
its tests and deviations. Parent manuscripts integrate the units. Mechanical
validation establishes integrity; only Sean Brady can approve an exact SAMA
revision. Current authority and public release are further separate
transitions. The present methodology and four volumes are built to terminate
at an exact-hash, unapproved handoff unless those later actions are explicitly
authorized.
