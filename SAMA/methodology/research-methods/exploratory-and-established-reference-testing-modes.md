## Conceptual abstract

SAM uses two differently typed testing modes. Exploratory mathematical research
is the default for new relations, operators, finite grammars and proof routes.
Established-reference testing is used when a SAM construction is compared with
measurements or an established physical calculation. The distinction changes
the technical contract of the test; it does not rank one mode above the other.

Exploration needs room for disciplined trial and error. An established-reference
comparison needs controls against target leakage, result-driven fitting and
post-result mutation. Importing the second mode's entire ceremony into every
new mathematical question would change the research process. Omitting it when
external targets are load-bearing would leave the comparison ill-typed.

## 1. Mode selection begins from the executable question

Let a campaign be summarized by

\[
\mathfrak T=(O, M, Q, C, B),
\]

where (O) is the conceptual object, (M) its technical map, (Q) the
executable question, (C) the controls and (B) the result boundary. The
testing mode is selected from (Q) and the provenance of its comparator.

Use exploratory mode when the central question has the form:

```text
Does this internally defined structure have the proposed identity,
invariant, factorization, sign, closure, transfer or computational behavior?
```

Use established-reference mode when it has the form:

```text
Does this fixed SAM construction contact or reproduce an independently
specified measurement or established calculation under a declared comparator?
```

The presence of numbers alone does not select the latter. Exact enumeration of
a new finite grammar remains exploratory unless an external reference is used
as the result-bearing target.

## 2. Exploratory mathematical research

### 2.1 Conceptual aim

Exploration asks what structure follows from the active definitions. Trial and
error is part of the derivation chain: a failed factorization can reveal the
missing type, a counterexample can locate the correct domain, and a wrong
control can expose an invariant.

### 2.2 Technical workflow

An exploratory campaign normally proceeds as follows:

1. Name the conceptual objects and their current source authority.
2. Translate them into explicit mathematical types.
3. State the candidate relation or executable question.
4. Choose controls that distinguish the proposed mechanism from the most
   relevant alternatives.
5. Implement the smallest exact or numerically controlled test that answers
   the question.
6. Preserve every material outcome, including counterexamples and defects.
7. Update the candidate route from what the execution actually exposed.
8. Assign a result classification only when the scoped result supports one.

Precommitments, hashes or independent replays may be used when they improve
custody, but exploratory legitimacy does not depend on a mechanical roster of
all possible procedures.

### 2.3 Result boundary

The output must name the tested domain. A finite enumeration establishes what
happened over that finite set. A symbolic derivation establishes what follows
from its stated premises. Neither should be expanded to a different domain by
rhetorical implication.

## 3. Established-reference testing

### 3.1 Conceptual aim

An established-reference test asks whether a fixed construction makes contact
with an independently meaningful value, curve, catalog or established
calculation. Examples include optical clocks, supernova distances, BAO,
recombination, CMB quantities and galaxy-halo comparisons.

### 3.2 Technical workflow

The comparator introduces additional dependencies, so the chain is expanded:

1. Record the reference value or dataset, its units, provenance and meaning.
2. Explain why that comparator answers the SAM question.
3. Freeze the SAM formula, constants, row set, selector order and permitted
   transformations before result inspection when fitting could contaminate the
   comparison.
4. Separate discovery/calibration data from holdout data when the test relies
   on transfer or prediction.
5. Declare the comparator, tolerances and statistical readouts before using
   them to grade the result.
6. Register wrong controls for target substitution, row deletion, formula
   patching, tolerance expansion and leakage when applicable.
7. Execute the fixed lane and preserve its full row-level or aggregate output.
8. Report the scoped outcome and every named open item.

The added discipline protects the meaning of “independent reference.” It is
not an invitation to add thresholds that the test did not declare.

## 4. The Last Campaign as a locked-replay instance

LC00 asks whether downstream branches replay from a locked primitive stack
without mutation. It fixes

\[
\alpha_H=2,\quad R=12,\quad D=3,\quad A_0=\frac1{\pi R},
\]

together with

\[
R^2=144,\quad 2^{-D}=\frac18,\quad \frac78,\quad
\frac{D^2}{2^D}=\frac98,\quad
\frac{D^2}{2^{D+1}}=\frac9{16},\quad
\frac{D^2}{R}=\frac34.
\]

The derivation of the campaign contract is direct:

```text
fixed primitive stack
→ downstream formula manifests
→ no formula/constant/row/label mutation
→ one replay lane at a time
→ lane-specific result and preserved controls
```

LC00 does not declare the downstream lanes passed merely because their premises
are registered. Each lane must execute and earn its own result. This is a
specific locked-replay architecture, not a universal prerequisite for every
exploratory mathematical campaign.

## 5. Hybrid and transition cases

A campaign can change mode across stages. A relation may be discovered
exploratorily, derived and debugged on internal examples, then frozen for a
separate established-reference holdout. The two stages should receive separate
artifacts:

```text
exploratory discovery artifact
→ fixed candidate definition
→ reference-test precommit
→ independent comparison artifact
```

This separation preserves legitimate learning during discovery while preventing
that learning from leaking into the claimed holdout.

## 6. Mode-selection failures

The common failures are type failures:

- treating every exploratory branch as though it were a locked observational
  campaign;
- treating an external target as a harmless internal debugging value;
- calling a tuned comparison a holdout;
- requiring a prior established model to authorize a new mathematical test;
- dropping comparator provenance because the formula is exact; or
- inheriting an old branch's procedure when the new question has a different
  result boundary.

The correction is to rewrite (mathfrak T=(O,M,Q,C,B)) explicitly and select
the mode from the executable question.

## 7. Current boundaries and open relations

The modes specify procedure, not outcome. Neither a frozen replay nor a creative
exploration implies success. The applicable SAM classification is assigned only
after execution under the declared boundary.
