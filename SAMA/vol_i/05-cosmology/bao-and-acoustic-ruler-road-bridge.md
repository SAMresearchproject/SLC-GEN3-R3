## Conceptual abstract

BAO does not consume one undifferentiated “distance.” A transverse ruler, a
radial ruler, an isotropic volume distance and an Alcock--Paczyński ratio each
apply a different projection after the common road is declared. The branch
therefore has two coupled but separately typed pieces:

1. a ruler bridge that maps the frozen acoustic source ruler into the drag
   ruler; and
2. a road/projection grammar that maps the cosmological luminosity road into
   the transverse and radial BAO families.

The ordinary projection is linear in its phase-like variable,

\[
\mathcal S_{\rm projection}(\phi)=1+w\phi,
\]

while the burst lane is

\[
\mathcal B(\phi)=1+\frac DR\phi^R.
\]

The exponentiated term cannot be moved into the ordinary projection. The
ruler weight is

\[
w=\frac DR\frac{\Omega_b}{\Omega_{\rm trapped}},
\]

and the typed bridge is

\[
r_{\rm drag}=r_*(1+w).
\]

This chapter also preserves a crucial deviation: the frozen typed bridge and
the later substrate-inventory SN/BAO/CMB packet emit two different drag-ruler
numbers under different dependencies. They are not alternative estimates to
average together. A later lift-rule audit failed cleanly and localized the
remaining compressed-acoustic discrepancy to the recombination layer.

## 3. Separate ordinary projection from burst closure

The ordinary projection lane is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal S_{\rm projection}(\phi)=1+w\phi
}.
\]

It changes linearly with \(\phi\):

\[
\frac{d\mathcal S_{\rm projection}}{d\phi}=w.
\]

The burst lane is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B(\phi)=1+\frac DR\phi^R
}.
\]

Its derivative is

\[
\frac{d\mathcal B}{d\phi}
=\frac DR R\phi^{R-1}=D\phi^{R-1}.
\]

For \(R=12\), the response is highly suppressed for \(|\phi|<1\) and becomes
closure-sensitive near unit phase. That is why \(\phi^{12}\) belongs to a
burst lane, not the ordinary projection. Replacing \(w\phi\) by
\((D/R)\phi^R\) would change the functional family before any BAO row is
evaluated.

## 5. Derive the BAO observable family

Let \(S(z)\) be the declared native source-distance curve and let

\[
f_A(z)=1-A_{\rm los}(z).
\]

The road-stretched luminosity family is

\[
D_{\rm SN}(z)=\frac{S(z)}{f_A(z)}.
\]

This is the inverse direction of the catalog-to-native map in
`SAMA-D000011`: a native source curve is mapped forward into its readout road.

### 5.1 Transverse projection

The transverse comoving BAO member is

\[
\boxed{
D_M(z)=\frac{D_{\rm SN}(z)}{1+z}
}.
\]

The \(1+z\) factor is an observable-family projection. It is not another
accumulation factor.

### 5.2 Radial derivative projection

The radial member is the derivative of the transverse road:

\[
\boxed{
D_H(z)=\frac d{dz}\left[\frac{D_{\rm SN}(z)}{1+z}\right]
}.
\]

Apply the quotient/product rule:

\[
D_H(z)
=\frac{D_{\rm SN}'(z)}{1+z}
-\frac{D_{\rm SN}(z)}{(1+z)^2}.
\]

Now differentiate \(D_{\rm SN}=S/f_A\). Because

\[
f_A'(z)=-A_{\rm los}'(z),
\]

we obtain

\[
D_{\rm SN}'(z)
=\frac{S'(z)}{f_A(z)}
+\frac{S(z)A_{\rm los}'(z)}{f_A(z)^2}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
D_H
=\frac1{1+z}
\left[
\frac{S'}{f_A}+\frac{SA_{\rm los}'}{f_A^2}
\right]
-\frac{S}{f_A(1+z)^2}
}.
\]

The term proportional to \(A_{\rm los}'\) is the load-bearing derivative
extra. A speed-only multiplication by \(1/f_A\) cannot reproduce it.

### 5.3 Isotropic and anisotropic combinations

The volume-averaged distance is

\[
\boxed{
D_V(z)=\left[zD_M(z)^2D_H(z)\right]^{1/3}
}.
\]

The Alcock--Paczyński ratio is

\[
\boxed{
F_{AP}(z)=\frac{D_M(z)}{D_H(z)}
}.
\]

The drag ruler cancels from \(F_{AP}\). A common multiplicative speed factor
at one redshift also largely cancels; the radial derivative remains decisive.
This is why \(F_{AP}\) is the sharp control on correct observable projection.
