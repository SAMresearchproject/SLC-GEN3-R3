## Correction

Routes CR001b as the nonequilibrium Peebles appeal that improves the operator while exposing the 3.4 eV and optical-depth a-power defects.

**Question:** Does the Peebles three-level transport close the thermal ladder when the precommitted 3.4 eV net factor and 1/(a²H) optical depth are executed as written?

**Calculation:** Integrate the declared Peebles ODE, then integrate the precommitted optical depth with 1/(a²H) and compute the acoustic outputs.

**Recorded outcome:** CR001b exposes both defects and, while honoring its precommit, records z*=227.3151 with all three acoustic gates failing. The failed appeal remains the correction-stage predecessor to CR001c.

**Scope of this result:** This appeal identifies implementation faults but does not itself supply the corrected native result.

**Controls:**

- Unchanged acoustic gates and source-custody checks.

**Diagnostic comparisons:**

- 3.4 eV used as the net hydrogen recombination factor.
- Conformal-distance 1/(a²H) used in a proper-time optical-depth integral.
