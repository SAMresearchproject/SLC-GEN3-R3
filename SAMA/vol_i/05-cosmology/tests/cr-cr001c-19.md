## Retest

Routes CR001c as the corrected same-lane retest with the 13.6 eV net factor, proper-time 1/(aH) optical depth and sealed H0 chronology intact.

**Question:** With the net factor corrected to 13.6 eV and optical depth derived from dt=da/(aH), do all three acoustic gates pass under the unchanged sealed inputs?

**Calculation:** Integrate corrected Peebles transport, solve tau(a*)=1 and tau_d(ad)=1, then compute the sound horizon, angular scale and drag ruler.

**Recorded outcome:** CR001c records z*=1073.0632, zd=1055.8115, 100 theta*=1.0589, ell_A=296.6827 and r_d=144.8064 Mpc with all three gates passing. The Volume I spine assigns: The test result suggests strong contact with the concept.

**Project classification:** The test result suggests strong contact with the concept.

**Scope of this result:** The sealed test uses H0=68.76 and a corrected Peebles approximation; later CR036 chronology and full precision recombination remain separate.

**Controls:**
