## Conceptual abstract

The Higgs and neutrino lanes are short enough to look like isolated formulas.
They are not. Each is an ordered packet whose intermediate types determine
what the final number means.

The Higgs lane has two packets. LC02 begins from the radix-square domain,
releases its one-eighth carrier side, retains \(126\), and subtracts the
surface debit \(D^2/R=3/4\) to obtain \(125.25\). CR279 begins from a later
source-native Higgs mass in MeV, attaches the resolved role \(q_H=3\), applies
the bounce factor \(1/(64\pi)\), and emits a direct \(A\)-source value. The
packets are related by their Higgs ontology, but they have different inputs,
operators, units and historical burdens. Combining them into one formula
would destroy rather than simplify the construction.

The neutrino lane separates shape from scale. Its substrate-derived content is
the ratio packet

\[
1:\sqrt{\alpha_H}:\alpha_HD=1:\sqrt2:6.
\]

Squaring and differencing cancels a common positive scale and gives the exact
mass-splitting ratio \(35\). One measured large-splitting value then fixes the
absolute eV scale. The resulting \(8.477\), \(11.988\) and \(50.861\) meV
values inherit normal ordering because \(1<\sqrt2<6\). The ratio and ordering
are structural outputs; the absolute eV scale remains an external anchor.

## 1. Packet discipline

A packet is an ordered typed construction:

\[
\mathcal P
=
(\text{premises},\text{operator order},\text{units},
\text{controls},\text{boundary},\text{output}).
\]

Two packets may concern the same named particle without being interchangeable.
Likewise, two expressions can have the same dimensions without having the
same provenance.

For this chapter:

\[
\mathcal P_{H,\rm retained}
\ne
\mathcal P_{H,A\rm -source},
\]

and

\[
\mathcal P_{\nu,\rm ratio}
\ne
\mathcal P_{\nu,\rm absolute\ scale}.
\]

The joins are explicit:

\[
\mathcal P_{H,\rm retained}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{shared Higgs ontology}
\longleftrightarrow
\mathcal P_{H,A\rm -source},
\]

\[
\mathcal P_{\nu,\rm ratio}
+\text{one external positive scale}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P_{\nu,\rm masses\ and\ ordering}.
\]

Neither join authorizes a collapse of the two inputs into one untyped number.

## 3. Higgs retained-parent derivation

### 3.1 Carrier release and retention

Begin with

\[
R^2=12^2=144
\]

and

\[
2^{-D}=2^{-3}=\frac18.
\]

The released carrier side is

\[
R^2\,2^{-D}
=144\left(\frac18\right)
=18.
\]

The retained fraction is

\[
1-2^{-D}
=1-\frac18
=\frac78.
\]

Therefore the retained parent is

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{\rm native}
&=R^2(1-2^{-D})\\
&=144\left(\frac78\right)\\
&=18\cdot7\\
&=126.
\end{aligned}
\]

The carrier result also meets the independent structural identity

\[
\alpha_HD^2=2\cdot3^2=18.
\]

This arithmetic weld checks the release route. It does not turn the released
\(18\) into Higgs matter.

### 3.2 Surface debit

The retained parent is not yet the reveal. LC02 applies the declared surface
debit

\[
\delta_H=\frac{D^2}{R}.
\]

Substitution gives

\[
\delta_H
=\frac{3^2}{12}
=\frac9{12}
=\frac34.
\]

The reveal is then

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{\rm reveal}
&=H_{\rm native}-\delta_H\\
&=126-\frac34\\
&=\frac{504-3}{4}\\
&=\frac{501}{4}\\
&=125.25.
\end{aligned}
\]

The operation order is load-bearing:

\[
144
\xrightarrow{\rm retain\ 7/8}
126
\xrightarrow{\rm debit\ 3/4}
125.25.
\]

Subtracting the debit from \(144\) first would produce \(143.25\), a
differently typed and unauthorized route.

## 4. Higgs direct \(A\)-source weld

CR279 begins from a later source-native quantity

\[

=125077.36096514532\ {\rm MeV}.
\]

Its source is a G745c scalar-formula output; the measured Higgs target is not
used to select the value. The resolved role and bounce factor are

\[
q_H=3,
\qquad
r_{\rm bounce,H}
=\frac1{64\pi}.
\]

Numerically,

\[
r_{\rm bounce,H}
\approx0.004973591971621730.
\]

The direct weld is

\[
q_{A,H}
=m_{H,\rm native}
\left(1+r_{\rm bounce,H}\right)
=m_{H,\rm native}
\left(1+\frac1{64\pi}\right).
\]

Expanding the output separates antecedent and source increment:

\[
q_{A,H}
=m_{H,\rm native}
+\frac{m_{H,\rm native}}{64\pi}.
\]

With the source value,

\[
q_{A,H}
=125699.44472347321\ {\rm MeV},
\]

and

\[
\frac{q_{A,H}}{m_{H,\rm native}}
=1+\frac1{64\pi}
\approx1.004973591971621838.
\]

The source increment is therefore approximately

\[
125699.44472347321-125077.36096514532
=622.08375832789\ {\rm MeV}.
\]

This packet does not re-run the \(126\to125.25\) subtraction. It applies a
multiplicative \(A\)-source weld to a later MeV-valued antecedent.

## 6. Neutrino ratio, scale and ordering

### 6.1 Substrate ratio packet

CR001@20 selects

\[
r_1=1,
\qquad
r_2=\sqrt{\alpha_H}=\sqrt2,
\qquad
r_3=\alpha_HD=2\cdot3=6.
\]

Let a common positive scale \(b_\nu\) give

\[
m_i=b_\nu r_i.
\]

Then

\[
m_1=b_\nu,
\qquad
m_2=b_\nu\sqrt2,
\qquad
m_3=6b_\nu.
\]

### 6.2 The common scale cancels from the splitting ratio

The large splitting is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta m^2_{31}
&=m_3^2-m_1^2\\
&=b_\nu^2(r_3^2-r_1^2)\\
&=b_\nu^2(6^2-1^2)\\
&=35b_\nu^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

The small splitting is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta m^2_{21}
&=m_2^2-m_1^2\\
&=b_\nu^2(r_2^2-r_1^2)\\
&=b_\nu^2(2-1)\\
&=b_\nu^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

Taking the quotient cancels the external scale:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\Delta m^2_{31}}{\Delta m^2_{21}}
&=\frac{35b_\nu^2}{b_\nu^2}\\
&=35\\
&=\frac{(\alpha_HD)^2-1}{\alpha_H-1}.
\end{aligned}
\]

This is the precise substrate-derived content of
SAMA-C000160-R001. The source comparison value is \(33.8949\), so the
recorded relative deviation is

\[
\frac{35-33.8949}{33.8949}
\approx0.03260
=3.260\%.
\]

The ratio was evaluated against the source-declared 10% condition and
retained by the source artifact. No eV scale enters this quotient.

### 6.3 One external anchor sets the absolute scale

CR001@20 uses

\[
\Delta m^2_{31}
=2.515\times10^{-3}\ {\rm eV}^2
\]

as the external large-splitting anchor. Since

\[
\Delta m^2_{31}=35b_\nu^2,
\]

solve for the scale:

\[
\begin{aligned}
b_\nu
&=\sqrt{\frac{\Delta m^2_{31}}{35}}\\
&=\sqrt{\frac{2.515\times10^{-3}}{35}}\ {\rm eV}\\
&=0.0084768593\ {\rm eV}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The three masses follow:

\[
m_1=b_\nu
\approx8.477\ {\rm meV},
\]

\[
m_2=\sqrt2\,b_\nu
\approx11.988\ {\rm meV},
\]

\[
m_3=6b_\nu
\approx50.861\ {\rm meV}.
\]

Their total is

\[
\Sigma m_\nu
=m_1+m_2+m_3
\approx71.326\ {\rm meV}.
\]

The smaller splitting is a derived value:

\[
\Delta m^2_{21}=b_\nu^2
\approx7.186\times10^{-5}\ {\rm eV}^2,
\]

compared in the source with \(7.420\times10^{-5}\ {\rm eV}^2\), a recorded
deviation of about \(-3.16\%\).

The scale separation is exact:

\[
\underbrace{1:\sqrt2:6}_{\text{substrate ratio}}
\quad+\quad
\underbrace{\Delta m^2_{31}}_{\text{one external scale anchor}}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\underbrace{m_1,m_2,m_3}_{\text{absolute eV values}}.
\]

SAMA-C000161-R001 therefore does not call the absolute scale substrate
derived.

### 6.4 Normal ordering

Because

\[
1<\sqrt2<6
\]

and

\[
b_\nu>0,
\]

multiplication by \(b_\nu\) preserves the strict order:

\[
b_\nu<\sqrt2\,b_\nu<6b_\nu.
\]

Hence

\[
m_1<m_2<m_3.
\]

CR002@20 records normal ordering under the sealed ratio packet and its positive
scale. This conclusion is conditional on the packet's index assignment and
does not turn the external scale into a structural primitive.

### 6.5 Neutrino boundaries

The source packet retains several exact limits:

- one external measurement supplies the eV scale;
- the \(1:\sqrt2:6\) reading is not promoted as the only conceivable
  structurally motivated selector;
- Dirac versus Majorana mechanism is not selected;
- mixing structure is not derived by these two records;
- the mass-splitting ratio is the scale-free output; and
- normal ordering follows from the indexed positive ratios rather than an
  independent fitted ordering coefficient.
