## Conceptual abstract

Theta18 is the first overflow and the principal tensor-carrier candidate in
the tested SAM grammar. It is the released one-eighth lane of the 144 contact
budget, the 18-slot overlap in the two-sided 81/81 closure construction, the
difference between 144 and 126, the difference between 162 and 144, and the
normalization quantum that sends the retained/native/full capacities to the
ordered ladder 7|8|9.

These equalities describe one repeated carrier role, but they do not erase
type. Theta18 is not X1, matter, rest mass, the inactive A-field row proxy or
an ordinary p12 fee. Its directional address has 262,144 states,

\[
2^{18}=512\cdot512=4^9=|\mathbb Z_4^9|,
\]

with the two 512 views coupled by modulo-four carry. The factorization is an
address identity, not two independent 512-case machines.

The return route begins from M126 retention. A fee-exempt Theta18 lift yields
the independent N144 state, and same/reversed L162 images place those
`2^144` states inside a constrained `2^162` ambient occurrence surface.
L162 is therefore a readout ceiling, not 162 independent spins. The complete
chain preserves carrier, state, sidecar and readout as different objects.

## 1. Opening question and conceptual picture

The central question is:

> What carries a completed finite matter write back into the directional
> computation surface, and how does that carrier enlarge state without being
> counted as matter or folded into the X1 response?

The source-bound answer is a typed route:

```text
two N72 custody packets
  -> M126 retained matter/output state
  -- independent fee-exempt Theta18 lift -->
N144 independent union state
  -- same/reversed occurrence imaging and typed mismatch sidecar -->
N144, N144+d or L162 readout
```

The carrier is active in the transition while remaining distinct from both
the retained matter state and the response interface. D39 supplies X1 as the
transient response channel. This document assigns the tested moving-carrier
burden to Theta18.

## 2. Exact derivation of the carrier constants

### 2.1 From the SAM primitives to Theta18

Start with the registered substrate primitives

\[
\hat h=2,
\qquad D=3,
\qquad R=\hat h^2D=12,
\]

and the substrate and resolved-state values

\[
S=\hat h^D=8,
\qquad W=D^2=9.
\]

Theta follows in two exact ways:

\[
\Theta=\hat hW=2\cdot9=18,
\]

and

\[
\Theta=\frac{R^2}{S}=\frac{12^2}{8}=\frac{144}{8}=18.
\]

The agreement is not a new type assignment. It shows that the same 18-valued
carrier can be read from the resolved-state route and from the one-eighth
partition of the 144 contact domain.

### 2.2 Retained, native and full capacities

Let

\[
N=R^2=144.
\]

Removing the one-eighth carrier lane gives retained matter/output capacity

\[
M=N-\Theta=144-18=126=7\Theta.
\]

Adding the same carrier lane above N gives the full constrained readout

\[
L=N+\Theta=144+18=162=9\Theta.
\]

The complete normalized ladder is then

\[
\frac{M}{\Theta}=\frac{126}{18}=7,
\qquad
\frac{N}{\Theta}=\frac{144}{18}=8,
\qquad
\frac{L}{\Theta}=\frac{162}{18}=9,
\]

with the release check

\[
\frac{\Theta}{\Theta}=1.
\]

The numerical sequence aligns with retained seven-eighths, unresolved S8,
resolved W9 and the normalized X1 scalar occurrence. The alignment does not
make Theta18 identical to X1: Theta is the carrier quantum used in the
normalization; X1 remains the response channel in the lifecycle.

### 2.3 The coupled directional address

Theta18 also fixes the exact directional address size:

\[
2^{18}=262{,}144.
\]

Because

\[
262{,}144=512^2=(2^9)^2=2^{18}=4^9,
\]

the same address surface can be encoded as two 512-sided views or as nine
modulo-four coordinates:

\[
\mathbb Z_4^9.
\]

The equality is operational only with carry preserved. Signed translation in
one coordinate can propagate through the packed modulo-four representation;
the two 512 projections therefore cannot be executed as unrelated factors and
recombined afterward. The historical directional control reports that such a
split changes 1,179,648 direction/record cases. The correct object is one
coupled 18-bit address.

## 3. Type system for Theta return

### 3.1 Carrier-only and fee-exempt

## 4. M126 to N144 to L162, step by step

### 4.1 Retain two N72 custody packets as M126

The structured application begins with two exact N72 custody packets. Their
full source information is not treated as 144 unconstrained matter variables.
The declared retention operator produces M126: 126 independent retained
matter/output coordinates. The missing 18 coordinates are not lost data; they
are carried as the separately typed released Theta lane.

At the state-count level, the retained state contains

\[
2^{126}
\]

configurations.

### 4.2 Apply the independent carrier lift

The fee-exempt carrier has `2^18` directional addresses. Taking the independent
product yields

\[
2^{126}\cdot2^{18}=2^{144}.
\]

This is the N144 union state. The product is valid because the carrier is an
independent lift at this seam. It would be invalid to fold the multiplicity
into X1 or count Theta18 as 18 more matter rows.

### 4.3 Form same and reversed L162 images

The application then constructs two occurrence views—same and reversed—inside
an ambient 162-coordinate ledger. Both views retain exactly the `2^144` valid
N144 states. Thus

\[
|\operatorname{image}_{same}|=|\operatorname{image}_{reversed}|=2^{144},
\]

even though the formal ambient ceiling is

\[
2^{162}.
\]

This is the difference between readout arity and independent rank. The L162
slots can record where an occurrence appears; they are not 162 freely
selectable physical spins.

### 4.4 Route the mismatch sidecar

For a declared fixture, define

\[
d_t=\operatorname{popcount}(\text{same-mismatch mask}).
\]

The phase-aware application route is

```text
d=0       -> N144
0<d<18    -> N144+d
d=18      -> L162
```

The route preserves the state/readout distinction:

1. no mismatch means the N144 state is already sufficient;
2. a partial mismatch appends only the active defect coordinates;
3. a complete 18-coordinate mismatch reaches the L162 occurrence view.

Held-out fixtures receive exact extension rather than truncation. The sidecar
is application data, not a new global SLC state-space definition.
