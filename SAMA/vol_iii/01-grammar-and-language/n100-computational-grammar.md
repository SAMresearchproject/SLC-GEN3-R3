## Conceptual abstract

N100 is an exact frustrated-spin computation whose favorable computational
structure comes from its source grammar. The mathematical object is the
unchanged 100-spin, 260-edge Hamiltonian with

\[
2^{100}=1{,}267{,}650{,}600{,}228{,}229{,}401{,}496{,}703{,}205{,}376
\]

binary assignments. The first complete H14F route returns the full exact
density-of-states tensor in 3.828137395 seconds. A later structure-first route
detects eight conditional components, executes 165,920 local assignments and
convolves them to the coefficientwise-identical tensor in 0.500951441 seconds.
The reduced workload is an exact evaluation strategy; it does not reduce the
state space, change the Hamiltonian, sample the assignments or request only a
ground state.

The 100-row census is itself source-bound. The visible QP lane contains 105
records. Exactly one of the 21 complete five-role packet exclusions—the
`p=9,g=0` packet—produces 100 independently counted rows with exact native
inventory 16,200 and mean 162. The five excluded-count W9 witnesses remain
visible as derived `X1+W8` receipts. The A field remains present as non-row
infrastructure, and X1 remains non-row/non-matter. Those are three different
boundaries.

The central computational finding is not merely the observed wall time. The
source grammar contains exact decomposition certificates, and the compiler can
use them while preserving the complete result. Its workload also returns the
ledger quantity L162 through an independent arithmetic identity. The N105,
N81 and N72 controls show exactly which parts depend on the source grammar and
which do not.

## 1. Opening question and conceptual picture

The governing question is:

> Can a source-derived 100-row grammar expose enough exact structure to
> compute the complete `2^100` density-of-states tensor without visiting every
> assignment one by one, while returning exactly the same mathematical
> output?

The answer is represented by two equivalent exact routes:

```text
source-derived N100 Hamiltonian
  -> complete H14F variable-elimination route
  -> exact retained-role DOS tensor

same unchanged Hamiltonian
  -> detect exact conditional components
  -> compute each local exact tensor
  -> exact tensor/polynomial convolution
  -> coefficientwise-identical retained-role DOS tensor
```

The grammar does not replace the calculation. It tells the exact solver how
the complete sum can be factorized.

## 2. Definitions and cardinality firewall

### 2.1 Exact frustrated-spin density of states

For binary spins

\[
\sigma_i\in\{-1,+1\},\qquad i=1,\ldots,N,
\]

and a declared weighted graph, the exact Hamiltonian has the form

\[
H(\sigma)
=\sum_{(i,j)\in E}J_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j
+\sum_i h_i\sigma_i.
\]

The density of states is the integer function

\[
g(E)=\#\{\sigma\in\{-1,+1\}^N:H(\sigma)=E\}.
\]

Completeness requires

\[
\sum_Eg(E)=2^N.
\]

For N100, `N=100`. Frustration changes the organization of energy and the
ground sector; it does not change the formal assignment count.

### 2.2 Four quantities that must not be merged

| Quantity | Exact value | Type |
|---|---:|---|
| N100 source rows | 100 | independent spins of the declared Hamiltonian |
| complete configuration space | `2^100` | every formal binary assignment counted by the returned tensor |
| structure-first work | 165,920 | local assignments evaluated before exact convolution |
| L162 native ledger mean | 162 | source-ledger/accounting identity, not a coupling or state count |

The statement “exact `2^100`” refers to the tensor's complete count. It does
not say the runner iterated through `2^100` bit strings. Conversely, the
165,920 workload is not a claim that the physical or mathematical problem had
only 165,920 states.

### 2.3 The row-census exclusions are separately typed

The N100 census is produced after one historical five-row packet is excluded
from independent content. Preserve these three boundaries:

```text
p=9,g=0 packet: five derived W9 witnesses, visible but non-counted
A-field row: excluded because A is active non-row infrastructure
X1: response channel, not a physical/source row
```

Similar arithmetic around the 163-to-162 ledger does not make the three
objects equivalent.

### 2.4 Source values are not Hamiltonian couplings

The QP rows supply typed identity, topology and role information through a
separately declared compiler. Their native numerical values are not inserted
as `J_ij` or `h_i` merely because they occur on the same record. The tested
Hamiltonian is the source-derived 100-spin, 260-edge graph fixed before the
structure-first route.

## 4. Constructing the exact N100 problem

### 4.1 Freeze the graph and requested output

The declared problem contains:

- 100 independent binary spins;
- 260 source-derived edges;
- induced width 5 under the complete H14F route;
- a retained-role conditional DOS tensor;
- a 16-row scalar closure; and
- exact integer coefficients reconstructed across four CRT primes.

The requested output is the complete tensor, not only the minimum energy or a
small collection of moments. The graph, source roles and tensor schema are
fixed before either timing result.

### 4.2 Why exact routes can be smaller than the state count

The complete sum

\[
g(E)=\sum_{\sigma\in\{-1,+1\}^{100}}
\mathbf 1\{H(\sigma)=E\}
\]

can be rearranged without approximation. Variable elimination sums internal
spins while retaining exact boundary spectra. Disconnected conditional
components can be computed independently and convolved. Modular NTT lanes can
multiply polynomials exactly, and CRT reconstructs the integer coefficients
once the modulus product exceeds the coefficient range.

All such routes remain exact if they reproduce every coefficient and close the
complete state count.

## 6. Structure-first route from components to the same tensor

### 6.1 Detect exact conditional components

The source grammar exposes eight components with sizes

\[
15,15,15,15,15,10,10,5.
\]

They are conditional components: any shared retained port variables remain
explicit, while the interior variables of each component can be summed
locally.

### 6.2 Compute local exact tensors

For component `c` and retained boundary assignment `b`, define its exact
energy polynomial

\[
P_{c,b}(x)=\sum_E g_{c,b}(E)x^E.
\]

Exhaustive local work for the eight interiors is

\[
W_{100}
=5\cdot2^{15}+2\cdot2^{10}+2^5.
\]

Evaluate each term:

\[
5\cdot2^{15}=5\cdot32{,}768=163{,}840,
\]

\[
2\cdot2^{10}=2{,}048,
\qquad2^5=32,
\]

so

\[
W_{100}=163{,}840+2{,}048+32=165{,}920.
\]

### 6.3 Convolve rather than discard cross-component sums

For each retained boundary assignment, multiply the local polynomials:

\[
P_b(x)=\prod_{c=1}^{8}P_{c,b}(x).
\]

Polynomial multiplication performs the full energy convolution: every choice
of one local state from each component contributes to the coefficient at the
sum of their energies. Summing or assembling over the retained boundary
assignments returns the same retained-role tensor as the complete route.

No state is sampled or dropped. Factorization evaluates the product structure
of the full sum.

### 6.4 Compare complete outputs

The structure-first wall is 0.500951441 seconds. Relative to the 3.828137395
second H14F route, the observed ratio is approximately

\[
\frac{3.828137395}{0.500951441}\approx7.642.
\]

The load-bearing comparison is stronger than timing: the complete tensors
have the same coefficient hash and match coefficient by coefficient. The
Hamiltonian, state space, energy support and output schema are unchanged.

## 7. Grammar-to-work identity, derived line by line

The source ledger supplies

\[
N100_{native}=100\cdot162=16{,}200.
\]

The exact component work can be regrouped as

\[
165{,}920=165{,}888+32=162\cdot1{,}024+32.
\]

Therefore

\[
W_{100}=162\cdot2^{10}+2^5.
\]

Subtract the residual and divide by the ten-bit block:

\[
\frac{W_{100}-2^5}{2^{10}}
=\frac{165{,}920-32}{1{,}024}
=\frac{165{,}888}{1{,}024}
=162.
\]

There is a second exact form. Because

\[
72^2=5{,}184=32\cdot162,
\]

we have

\[
W_{100}=2^5(72^2+1)=32(5{,}184+1)=165{,}920.
\]

The residual `2^5` is the binary assignment space of the surviving five-role
`p=9,g=1` packet. It is not X1, Theta18 or a physical closure object. The
identity connects two independently sourced structures—the row ledger and the
exact workload—but the value 16,200 is not inserted into the Hamiltonian and
does not mechanically cause the runtime.

look like N100. This control prevents the source-structure finding from being

an unchanged parent rather than a retuned benchmark.
