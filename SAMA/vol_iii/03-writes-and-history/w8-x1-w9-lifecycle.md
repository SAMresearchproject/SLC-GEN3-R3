## Conceptual abstract

The W8–X1–W9 lifecycle describes one reciprocal transition, not three freely
interchangeable objects. W8 is the unresolved eight-position substrate state.
B/contact prepares the request-side transition. X1 is the one transient
reciprocal response channel. W9 is the resolved composite state or terminal
receipt of the completed relation,

\[
W9=X1+W8.
\]

The equality is typed. It cannot be read as ordinary inventory addition, as a
claim that X1 is matter, or as an equation saying B and X1 are two scalar
halves. The exact program surface can require B and X1 as differently typed
inputs and return W9 while a physical causal weld remains open. That is not a
defect in the lifecycle. It is the reason the lifecycle is useful: syntax,
execution, receipt, carrier and physical identification can be developed
without being collapsed into one assertion.

The evidence chain is cumulative. CR120V rejected the literal two-half entity
formula while retaining a typed outward/return relay. CR120W then preserved a
failed attempt to weld the A-field carrier directly to X1. CR120X corrected
the route by using Theta18 as a carrier-normalization quantum while keeping
X1 as the response type, and CR120Y stress-tested that separation. CR120Z and
CR120ZB carried the ordered B-then-X1 grammar into exact and finite-shot
quantum sandboxes, but neither installed physical definitions of B or X1.

## 1. Opening question and conceptual picture

The governing question is:

> How can an unresolved substrate state receive contact, return reciprocal
> information and finish as a resolved write while retaining the identity of
> the request, response, carrier and receipt?

The compact picture is

```text
W8 unresolved source/site state
  -- B/contact prepares the request -->
X1 transient reciprocal response
  -- typed resolution -->
W9 resolved composite state and retained receipt
```

This picture contains two related but nonidentical descriptions.

1. **State description.** W8 and W9 name the unresolved and resolved ends of
   the relation.
2. **Event description.** B/contact and X1 name the differently typed
   request-side and response-side participation needed to traverse between
   those ends.

The lifecycle therefore keeps endpoint state separate from the event that
produced it. A terminal W9 receipt records that the typed route completed; it
does not turn W9 into a new independent source object. Similarly, X1 can be
required by the type system without the installed runtime exposing an
independently variable X1 payload.

## 3. Typed lifecycle construction from start to finish

### 3.1 The formal operator premise

The language-level premise separates preparation from response. In G06, B
contains a binary flip but is not exhausted by that flip, while X1 has its own
frozen response matrix. The executable checks proceed in four stages:

1. resolve both operators through the single formal registry;
2. verify their frozen hashes and declared input/output shapes;
3. apply B to every declared prepare/request column and X1 to every response
   column; and
4. apply the involutions and recover the source basis exactly.

The hash, column, mapping and involution checks all pass. Exchanging request
and response, reducing B to its binary-flip suboperation, or selecting an
unfrozen sign/gauge would test a different operator surface.
