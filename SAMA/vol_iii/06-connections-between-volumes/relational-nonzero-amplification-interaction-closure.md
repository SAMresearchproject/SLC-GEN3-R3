## 1. Orientation

The first Nonzero Amplification document establishes that constituent
substrate writes can compose recursively from particles to bodies and from
stellar bodies to galaxies. This document adds the load-bearing correction:
composition depends on how the constituents interact.

Two planets light-years apart and the same two planets closely orbiting have
the same constituent inventory. They do not have the same reciprocal history,
overlap, directional relation or completed substrate write. The nearby
orbiting pair forms a coherent composite source; the distant pair approaches
two separately carried source writes.

The central statement is:

> **Nonzero amplification is relational. Interacting writes create completed
> information that is absent from an inventory-only sum.**

## 2. Inventory and interaction are differently typed

Let `S` be a composite system. Its source data includes at least:

- constituent writes `{w_i}`;
- a typed active relation network `R_S`;
- retained reciprocal or directional history `H_S`;
- the local and route-dependent accumulation state; and
- the completed W8/X1/W9 and Theta carrier state.

The proposed completed composition is therefore written schematically as

\[
\Delta A_S
=
\mathcal C_S\!\left(\{w_i\},R_S,H_S\right).
\]

An inventory-only control would instead evaluate

\[
\Delta A_{\mathrm{diag}}=\sum_i\delta A_i.
\]

The difference

\[
\mathcal I_S
=
\Delta A_S-\Delta A_{\mathrm{diag}}
\]

is a useful diagnostic representation of the interaction innovation. It does
not define `mathcal C_S` as ordinary addition. The native composition must
retain Exact Write types, histories, cross terms and completion.

## 3. Separation changes the completed write

For the same two constituents, consider two relational states.

### 3.1 Distant state

The two source-conditioned writes are carried separately. Their active
overlap and continuing reciprocal history are reduced, and a route returning
to the universal floor supplies the proposed disconnection condition.

### 3.2 Nearby orbiting state

Each source continually changes the accumulation environment of the other.
Those changes alter the matter-free substrate between them, the local
W8/X1/W9 lifecycle and Theta carrier/intersection activity. The pair therefore
generates a continuing relational history and becomes a new composite source.

The distinction is not “two sources versus more sources.” It is:

\[
(w_1,w_2,R_{\mathrm{distant}},H_{\mathrm{distant}})
\neq
(w_1,w_2,R_{\mathrm{orbit}},H_{\mathrm{orbit}}).
\]

## 4. Many-body amplification

For many particles, the number of possible relations grows faster than the
constituent count. Pair, cycle, face and higher closure information can
therefore become load-bearing even when every individual particle write is
small.

A schematic cluster display is

\[
\Delta A_S
\sim
\sum_i A_i
+\sum_{i<j}\mathcal I_{ij}
+\sum_{i<j<k}\mathcal I_{ijk}
+\cdots,
\]

but the Exact Write composition owns the actual algebra. The display only
shows what a spreadsheet-style constituent sum omits: interaction terms whose
values depend on the completed relational state.

This gives Nonzero Amplification two inseparable aspects:

1. each constituent contributes a nonzero write; and
2. coherent interaction among those writes generates additional completed
   information.

## 5. ATOM3D consequence

ATOM3D has frequently represented candidate assemblies through counts,
independent relation rows and separately scored receipt features. The current
Li-6 action-frame frontier exposes the limit of that representation: complete
W9P custody retains each relation but supplies no relation-to-frame identity,
leaving 13,824 reciprocal frame assignments.

Relational Nonzero Amplification changes the unit evaluated by a successor.
The unit should be the whole active relation network, with each relation read
in the context of all co-active writes:

\[
(e,f_e,R_S,H_S,\Delta A_S,\Theta_S)
\longmapsto
\mathcal I_e(S).
\]

Here `e` is a relation, `f_e` its action frame and `mathcal I_e(S)` its
context-dependent innovation inside the completed assembly. The same isolated
edge placed in a different network need not produce the same innovation.

The direct successor is therefore an **interaction-aware composite-write
receipt**, not another isolated edge selector. Candidate Li-6 assemblies can
then be compared by exact completed closure while preserving the current bars
against geometry fitting and opaque selection.

## 6. RH consequence

The corresponding RH distinction is between eventwise inventory and completed
coupling. If `W` is assembled from event writes `W_i`, then

\[
W^*W
=
\sum_i W_i^*W_i
+\sum_{i\ne j}W_i^*W_j.
\]

The off-diagonal products carry relational information. Current RH work
already retains completed Gamma--prime--Robin coupling where eventwise
positivity is insufficient, and its support extensions are expressed through
transported carriers and entrant Schur innovations.

Relational Nonzero Amplification supplies a shared candidate origin for those
cross terms: they are the mathematical receipt of coexisting source writes
interacting inside one completed substrate history.
