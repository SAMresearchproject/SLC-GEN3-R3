# RH Completed Xi, D0, and the Exact Weil Form

## Conceptual abstract

The completed object is not the raw prime sum. It combines prime powers,
archimedean Gamma data, reciprocal completion, floor terms, endpoint moments,
and the declared admissible test space. The functional equation

\[
\xi(s)=\xi(1-s)
\]

fixes the reciprocal centering. The selected Weil form `Q_xi(h)` is evaluated
only after its channels have been completed.

## 1. Admissible domain

`D0` is the project domain whose endpoint moment conditions remove the
non-decaying common modes and make the completed form well typed. H000442
activates all `D0` from positive-semidefinite source `H/M` data. This domain is
not replaced by arbitrary sampled vectors, a finite-zero roster, or F81.

## 2. Completed channel discipline

The computation preserves:

\[
Q_\xi(h)=Q_{\rm prime}(h)+Q_{\Gamma}(h)+Q_{\rm endpoint}(h)+Q_{A_0}(h)
\]

as a schematic typed sum. The terms identify channels; the source calculation
must combine them before using cancellation or assigning a sign. In
particular, individual tail columns can diverge while a paired completed
current remains finite.

## 3. Finite and universal roles

Exact finite forms supply identities, congruences, counterfamilies, positive
sections, and source reconstructions. They are the executable architecture of
the universal statement, not a numerical substitute for it. The current
frontier reduces the completed first-contact sign to one source-rate
inequality developed in `SAMA-D000070` and `SAMA-D000073`.

## 4. Separate programs

F81 spectral work and SLC applications may share objects with the RH route,
but neither is a premise of the completed-Weil criterion. The public proof
claim, internal universal-sign assignment, and operational Q2 adapter are also
separate authority surfaces.
