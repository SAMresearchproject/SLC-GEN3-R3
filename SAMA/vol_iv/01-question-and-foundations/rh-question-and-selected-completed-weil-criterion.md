# RH Question and the Selected Completed-Weil Criterion

## Conceptual abstract

The Riemann Hypothesis states that every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta
function has real part one half. Volume IV does not begin by fitting or
enumerating zeros. It uses the completed function

\[
\xi(s)=\tfrac12s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),
\qquad \xi(s)=\xi(1-s),
\]

and one selected Weil-positivity formulation on a declared admissible test
space. The completed object is essential: the prime, archimedean, endpoint,
and floor contributions cannot be split into independently convergent claims
and then recombined informally.

The route has four typed stages:

```text
admissible test object
```
