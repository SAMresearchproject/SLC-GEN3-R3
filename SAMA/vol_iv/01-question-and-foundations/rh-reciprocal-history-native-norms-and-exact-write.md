## Conceptual abstract

Reciprocal History retains ordered paths that an endpoint or density-of-states
summary loses. On the N100 source graph, 260 undirected edges produce 520
oriented edge states. Nonbacktracking continuation separates reversal from
forward history; 10,700 oriented primitives pair into 5,350 reciprocal
classes. Their native norms carry the distinction between a route and its
reverse into later Write objects.

## 1. Preserved direct failure

The first direct scalar-history Euler-product identification is not retained:
its first coefficient is 100 rather than the ordinary-prime coefficient 2.
**The test falsifies the concept.** This failure prevents the graph history
from being renamed as the ordinary-prime Euler product.

The successor separates two jobs:

- the radix-12 first-singularity tower supplies the ordinary-prime ordering;
- the N100 role/depth grading supplies coefficient one, while N95 and N105
  controls return zero.

A nontrivial-zero ordinate does not select the tower.
