# RH PB1/PB2 — Factorization, W9P, Theta, and the AFC Defect

## Conceptual abstract

PB1 closes the source address, arbitrary-`D0` receipt, positive cutoff factor,
and completed receipt identity. The remaining pre-sign certificate is a lower
frame or Douglas contraction. Its project classification is **The test result
suggests the concept is possible.**

PB2 exposes the certificate as the difference between one complete history
Gram and one AFC defect.

## 1. Exact forms

For cutoff `D` and source state `X`,

\[
P_D=R_{D,X}^*R_{D,X},
\qquad
B_{D,X}^*B_{D,X}=c_X\Pi_D^0,
\]

and

\[
A_D=P_D-c_X\Pi_D^0.
\]

The equivalent cutoff-wide contraction target is

\[
B_{D,X}=C_{D,X}R_{D,X},
\qquad \|C_{D,X}\|\le1.
\]

This equivalence identifies the sign question without assuming the
factorization exists.

## 2. Ontology correction

```text
one complete W9_L + one complete W9_R = one ordered W9P
one completion = one Theta18
```

\[

\qquad

between the readout Gram and the AFC defect is false; dominance is exactly the
sign of `A_D`.
