# Adaptive carrier and Robin support closures

[Back to records](README.md)

Record identity: `SAMA-RH-R0014`. Record type: result.

**Recorded outcome:** Completion-adapted Schur carriers, zero-extension tail Grams and exact Robin tails close log8, log9 and log10, with backward restriction closing smaller supports.

**Recorded validation:** Primary and independent controls pass at each installed carrier and support stage.

**Scope of this result:** The installed support theorems do not state all-support closure or eventwise prime PSD.
