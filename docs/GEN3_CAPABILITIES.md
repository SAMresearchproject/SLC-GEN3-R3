# GEN3 capabilities

A capability map for the shared runtime and its research domains, documented **17 September 2026**.

GEN3 supplies the common execution, learning and retained history. The CE connects that runtime to Starbreaker (SB), A3D41/ATOM3D and RH through domain-specific source contracts.

| Layer | Installed research version | Role |
|---|---|---|
| GEN3 | SLC-GEN3-R4 / SLC-GEN3-CEV1-R4 | Shared computation, learning, storage and recovery |
| SB | SB-GEN3-ACCUMULATION-R1 | Signed reception, accumulated histories and receiver planning |
| A3D41 | A3D41-T18-CONTACT-R2 | Typed matter construction, contact actions, minima and inverse readouts |
| RH | RH-GEN3-GROWTH-V5 | Exact Möbius-source arithmetic, continuation and research selection |

These installed research versions use generation `GEN3-BETHE1-20260917-G1`. The repository also distributes the portable R3 package. The capabilities below describe the installed research system; the linked contracts and [reproduction guide](../research/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) identify the required environment. This documentation update does not replace the portable runtime.

## GEN3 — shared capabilities

| Capability | What it provides |
|---|---|
| Exact source execution | Rational and admitted real-quadratic arithmetic, with source identity, ordered basis, signed state, phase and alternatives retained. |
| History accounting | Exact rise, fall, total variation, net change and maximum relative excursion; preserves the original reference, event order and every maximizing tie. |
| Observation and inverse readout | Incorporates actual observations, narrows admitted alternatives and retains unresolved alternatives and ties. |
| Signed energy and common minima | Calculates signed parent energy, repayment and gain; finds complete minima for supplied finite cost rosters, their intersection and exact excess. |
| Learning and selection | Fits source-bound decision trees, returns exact probabilities and readable rules, ranks candidate constructions and retains tied rankings. Training, development and reserved-test roles remain distinct. |
| Certified logarithms | Computes rational bounds for scalar and spectral logarithms; retains omitted-mode allowances and supports incremental refinement and signed transport accounts. |
| Operator and Bethe algebra | Computes source words, invariant closure, recurrences, creation polynomials, terminal actions, module actions, products and local exchange-obstruction certificates. |
| Durable recovery | Stores inputs, results, models, source bindings and ordered histories under authenticated checkpoint roots; reuses retained answers and exports records without repeating native arithmetic. |

**Detailed contracts:** [Native energy, minima and learning](capabilities/GEN3_NATIVE.md) · [Certified logarithms](capabilities/CERTIFIED_LOG.md) · [Operator logarithms](capabilities/OPERATOR_LOG.md) · [Source operators](capabilities/SOURCE_OPERATORS.md) · [Bethe algebra and construction](capabilities/BETHE.md).

Exact operator products and obstruction certificates are available. General factorized scattering and full Bethe equations remain open research questions.

## SB / A3D41 — source and matter capabilities

### Starbreaker (SB)

| Capability | What it provides |
|---|---|
| Structured source histories | Retains seed, collapse and final stages, source support, contact events, retained matter and released carrier traffic. |
| Signed reception | Computes signed J4 receiver amplitudes and response-norm action while retaining phase and axis occupancy. |
| Accumulated history | Accounts for repeated source cycles, including positive variation when the endpoint returns to its original value; preserves event order and maximum ties. |
| Learned receiver planning | Applies retained construction policies and signed-energy gain models to source-bound proposals; exposes `SB_CONSTRUCTION_POLICY`, `SB_SIGNED_ENERGY` and `SB_RECEIVER_PLAN`. |
| GW encoding application | Supports the published carrier → modulation → signed residual → information tests → gated decoder workflow, including continuous source control and the three bounded follow-up experiments. |

**Research and results:** [Starbreaker](../research/starbreaker/README.md) · [GW encoding](../gw-encoding/README.md). Physical energy or strain units require an explicit source mapping; the GW scaling study records its assigned mapping.

### A3D41 / ATOM3D

| Capability | What it provides |
|---|---|
| Typed construction | Builds site, relation, phase and circulation inventories from declared source geometry. |
| Contact and common minima | Calculates exact contact actions, complete minimum sets, common minima and retained cover/placement alternatives. |
| Li-6 grammar and signed inverse | Reconstructs source currents and decodes the tested state, cover and placement configurations from independent signed responses. |
| Learned construction proposals | Uses retained construction policies and learned source-history templates to rank candidate families and continuations. |
| Complete construction evaluation | Evaluates the original finite construction roster for requested families and contexts, returning exact minimum action and the full 262,144-state minimum bitset per context. |
| Source algebra and exchange | Computes module closure, sparse polynomial products, terminal corrections and exact local exchange obstructions through the shared R4 engine. |

**Research and contracts:** [A3D41/ATOM3D](../research/atom3d/README.md) · [Construction planning](capabilities/GEN3_NATIVE.md#domain-adoption) · [Complete evaluation and algebra](capabilities/BETHE.md).

Construction outcomes retain their source and context. Physical binding, stability and dimensional energy assignments are separate from the finite construction calculation.

## RH — arithmetic research capabilities

| Capability | What it provides |
|---|---|
| Source and evidence admission | Admits authenticated research evidence and original stopped Möbius vectors, preserving normalization, source hashes and training/held-out roles. |
| Signed-divisor and intrinsic transport | Computes exact dual witnesses, resistance coordinates, flux, endpoint terms, signed cross terms and tails. |
| Forcing and prefix scans | Evaluates signed forcing identities and complete declared prefix scans; retains all maxima, ties and rejected candidates. |
| Incremental continuation | Reuses retained weighted state when advancing a prefix and evaluates only new energy admissions; separately records source-reconstruction and kernel work. |
| Conditional bounds and ancestry | Enumerates short-horizon branches using known prefix information, retains signed extrema and checks exact scale-ancestry identities. |
| Spectral profiles | Applies shared GEN3 spectral admission, logarithmic profiles and refinement to the original source, preserving the distinction between spectral and original-source parameters. |
| Research learning and selection | Fits native exact regression stumps to eligible training witnesses and ranks unused source candidates while retaining ties and model ancestry. |
| Hypotheses and recovery | Records finite hypothesis checks, conditional implications and failures; exports and recovers source records, results and models without new arithmetic. |

The RH-V5 package exposes **19 operations**. Source scales are dyadic from 2 through 8,192; conditional continuation supports horizons of 1–6 admissions, clipped at the source end. The whole-scale-4,096 source is held out of fitting.

**Detailed contract:** [RH-V5 operations and recovery](capabilities/RH_V5.md). **Research:** [RH overview and paper](../research/rh/README.md) · [Executable retained-history checks](../research/rh/check/README.md).

Finite certificates and conditional implications retain their stated scope. The uniform arithmetic estimates and full RH remain open.
