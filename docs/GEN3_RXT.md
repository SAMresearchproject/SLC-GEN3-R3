# GEN3-RXT native research deployment

**Snapshot: 14 September 2026.** GEN3-RXT-R7.1 is the native joint research
deployment on the SLC-GEN3-R3 / SLC-GEN3-CEV1-R3 foundation. Its scoped engines
are RH-GXT-R2 + RH-TRANSFER-R1, A3D41-RXT-R3 and SB-GEN3-RXT-R2. The managed RH
binding is RH-GEN3-GROWTH-V4.

One C++/CUDA/GMP engine owns exact domain computation and acquired memory.
CPU/GMP kernels perform exact source arithmetic; CUDA supplies construction and
supported policy work. Native source traces feed training and source-bound
selection. Python supplies orchestration and explicitly identified independent
checks. Research papers distinguish native calculations from those checks.

The completed R4 predecessor retains 1,590,144 exact construction families and
45 selected native policies. R7/R7.1 adds domain feedback, original RH weighted
cells, exact signed U/D/V/L/M, source transport, harmonic reconstruction and
route feedback. The retained R7 update includes 36,864 fresh families, 1,216
Starbreaker histories and 288 horizon cases, with 252 formed. These counts
identify their completed snapshots, not an unbounded capability claim.

## What the RH work uses

The weekend investigation progressed through original-kernel spectral readout,
signed divisor transport, full dyadic forcing energy and source-conditioned
admission bounds. Actual complete source traces and selected results are
retained. Learned selections, fixed full-scale scans, later refinements and
held-out data have separate provenance. The latest 8192 scan is fixed
complete-scale exploration; its later source calls refine that scan's maximum.

The [RH section](../research/rh/README.md) records the exact results and the
unchanged uniform signed-growth target. The [paper](../research/rh/paper.md)
includes the selection audit and current-state four-admission certificates.

## Relationship to this download

The repository's portable Python R3 runtime supports the documented core
operations and local checkpoint recovery. The GEN3-RXT C++/CUDA deployment,
large acquired stores, configured CE domains and GPU workers are separate
installations. The research section publishes their mathematical results and
source provenance. Installing the portable requirements does not launch the
pod engine or reproduce its hardware allocation.
