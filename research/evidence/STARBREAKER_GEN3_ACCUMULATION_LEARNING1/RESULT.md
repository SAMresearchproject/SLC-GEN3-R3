The earlier J4 simulation identified the missing source-action profile. The new
profile computes `A(k)=x(k)^2+y(k)^2` directly from the retained signed pre-Gram
receiver amplitudes, using native GEN3 exact arithmetic. This profile is a
Codex-defined response-norm readout of the existing source; it assigns no
physical energy scale. Its full signed amplitudes, packet addresses, relation,
direction, rho and source ancestry remain attached to every ordered point.

For adjacent positive actions the engine computes `delta=ln(A_after/A_before)`.
U accumulates positive changes, D the magnitudes of negative changes,
V=U+D and L=U-D. M retains the maximum excursion from the original reference
and every maximizing occurrence. These are exact logarithm objects whose
positive rational arguments compose by multiplication. Their values do not
depend on floating-point logarithm approximations. Missing/nonpositive inputs
retain GEN3's explicit gap semantics.

accumulated variation remains nonzero. A recovered second cycle doubles U,D,V
exactly and retains the original reference and all maximum ties.
