## Source action shared by the two channels

The new reception law has one common native clock. For a selected relation,
write its source components as real pairs v=(Re z,Im z) and a=|v|. A native
unit Write in direction d acts as

    v' = R_d v,     a' = P a,
    R_+ = [[0,-1],[1,0]],    R_- = [[0,1],[-1,0]],
    P = [[0,1],[1,0]].

The identity |R_d v|=P|v| gives the two-channel source action
diag(R_d,P) on (v,a). Both source maps therefore advance at the same native
event. The signed part records the four-phase cycle; axis occupancy records
